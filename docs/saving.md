# Saving

Ashen Wilds keeps one save slot. Your run continues from the title menu with **Continue**.

## When the game saves

- **Automatically at every dawn.**
- **From the pause menu:** *Save game*, or *Save and quit to title*.

## What is saved

The world itself is rebuilt from its seed, so the save only records what you changed:

- every structure you built, with its contents, fuel, battery charge and filter setting
- trees you felled, deposits you mined, chests you opened and bushes you picked
- Speed Racers and their fuel
- your position, health, hunger, stamina, active buffs and inventory
- sword upgrades, the day and time, raiders slain, and tutorial progress

Raiders on the map are not saved; a loaded game starts with none nearby.

## Death is permanent

When you die, your save is deleted. Starting a **New game** also replaces any existing save.
