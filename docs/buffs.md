# Buffs

Buffs are timed boosts. Each plays its own animation around you when gained and shows under your health bars with a countdown. Gaining a buff you already have resets its timer.

| Buff | How to get it | Lasts | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rested | Stand by one of your own campfires for about 4 seconds | 2 minutes | +20% movement speed, stamina refills 60% faster |
| Shielded | Eat a Serum | 45 seconds | Incoming damage is halved, rounded down, so most 1-damage hits are blocked entirely |
| Blazing | Eat an Elixir | 40 seconds | +2 sword damage, and every hit sets the target burning for 1 damage a second over 3 seconds |

Serum comes from the [Bio Lab](production.md) and Elixir from the [Alchemy Table](building.md). Both are also food, so pressing F eats them when they are the best food you carry (see [Survival](survival.md#food)).

Healing from food or regeneration plays a separate heal animation but is not a timed buff.

Starvation and other damage that does not come from a hit is not reduced by Shielded.
