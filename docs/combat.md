# Combat and enemies

Your only weapon is the sword. A slash deals 1 damage in a forward arc; a chop deals 3 in a wider arc with heavy knockback but costs 4 stamina. Each [forge upgrade](defense.md#sword-upgrades) adds +1 to both. Hold the attack button to chain slashes.

The same strikes fell trees, break rocks and mine deposits.

## The Dust Jumper (boss)

From day 3, the Dust Jumper may hunt you once a night. It has 40 health and a health bar at the top of the screen.

- **Swipe:** up close it swipes for 2 damage.
- **Leap and slam:** every few seconds it jumps out of sight. A target marker chases you, locks in place for a moment, then the boss crashes down for 3 damage around the marker. Roll or run out of the marker as it locks.
- **Rage:** below half health it leaps more often and calls in two Warriors after each slam.
- **Reward:** 5 Gold Ore, 4 Amethyst and 4 Cobalt Ore.

It cannot be hurt while airborne.
