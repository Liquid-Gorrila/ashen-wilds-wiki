# Caves

Cave entrances are dark rock mounds found in some regions of the desert, usually ringed by rich ore. Walk up to the mouth to enter.

Every cave leads to its own tunnel system, the same each time you visit. Inside you'll find:

| Find | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Rich ore seams | Gold, cobalt and amethyst (they glow in the dark), plus coal and copper. Each yields 2–4 ore. |
| Bone totems | Break them for bone. |
| Rich chests | Two, deep in the tunnels: gold, amethyst and cooked meat. |
| Tribe warriors | Five to eight Brutes, Tamed Beasts, Spear Guards, Hunters and Warriors. They stand dormant until you come close. |

## Rules

- Caves are dark. Your own light and the glowing seams are all you have.
- Night raids and sandstorms don't reach you underground, and there is no desert heat.
- Raiders chasing you on the surface give up when you go down.
- The compass shows only an **Exit** marker. The exit is the lit shaft where you arrived; walk up to it to climb out.
- Anything you mine or loot stays gone.
- Defeat every warrior in a cave to **silence** it: it stays empty of enemies from then on, and its entrance reads "(quiet)".
- Saving while underground puts you back at the cave entrance when you load.

Exploring and silencing a cave are both [objectives](objectives.md) in Part 6.
