# The world

Every run generates a new, endless world from a random seed, loaded in chunks around you as you travel. Most of it is desert; the living biomes exist only as small oases, typically 10 to 20 cells across.

You always start inside an oasis, at a small camp with a tent, a firepit, a supply chest and a [Speed Racer](speed-racer.md).

## Biomes

| Biome | Trees and plants | Animals | Ore found here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desert | Dead trees, bones, dry tufts | Rare boar | Stone, copper, coal |
| Scorched Badlands | Dead trees | None | Coal, gold |
| Greenwood Oasis | Green trees, berry bushes, flowers | Sheep, chicken, rabbit | Stone, copper |
| Birch Oasis | Birch trees, berry bushes | Rabbit, sheep | Stone, copper |
| Autumn Oasis | Autumn trees, berry bushes | Boar, chicken | Copper, gold |
| Crimson Oasis | Crimson trees, berry bushes | Boar | Gold, amethyst |
| Violet Oasis | Violet trees, berry bushes | Rabbit | Amethyst, cobalt |
| Frost Oasis | Teal trees, berry bushes | Rabbit, sheep | Cobalt, stone |

Desert and Badlands are hot by day (see [Survival](survival.md)). Each oasis is ringed by a fringe of scrubby sand.

## Terrain

- **Chasms** are bottomless gaps in the ground. You cannot cross them on foot or by racer.
- **Rock outcrops** are clusters of raised stone blocks on high ground. Break them for stone; ore deposits also sit among them.

## Points of interest

The world is divided into regions of 40 by 40 cells, and about two thirds of regions hold one landmark.

| Landmark | What you find |
| --- | --- |
| Camp | Tents, a lit firepit, a loot chest, baskets |
| Ruin | A sci-fi building, a loot chest, a lamp, sometimes a Speed Racer |
| Merchant camp | A trader, a tent, a lamp, sometimes a Speed Racer |
| Tribe camp | A large tribal tent, bone totems, a rich chest |
| Bone field | Giant bones, bone totems, a chest |
| Cave | A rock cave mouth ringed by rich ore (gold, cobalt, amethyst, copper). It leads underground: see [Caves](caves.md). |

Anything you take from the world stays taken: felled trees, mined deposits, opened chests and found racers never come back.
