# Release notes

## 1.0: Water and heat

The desert now fights back with heat as well as raiders, and water becomes something you build for.

**Heat and shade**

- A new **Cool** meter drains under the midday sun, fastest on scorching ground. See [Heat and shade](../survival/heat.md).
- Two new ailments: **Overheated** (slow stamina, faster thirst) and **Heatstroke** (lose health).
- New **Awning** (3 Wood, 2 Bone, no station needed): shade from the midday sun.
- Shade now comes only from an awning or from standing right up against a tall building, no longer from anywhere near your base.
- Drinking Water cools you, and drinking your fill at a pool cools you completely.

**Water**

- New **Well** (needs a workbench): slowly fills with water, faster in an oasis. See [Water](../survival/water.md).
- New **Condenser** (needs a component table, uses power): pulls water from the air, twice as fast at night.
- **Planters need water**: 1 Water grows a crop through to harvest. **Greenhouses** use 1 Water per harvest.
- Planters and greenhouses within about 4 tiles of a well or condenser water themselves.
- **Sandstorms silt pools:** you can still drink, but you can't fill waterskins.

**Tutorial**

- Six new goals: drink at the pool, build an awning, dig a well, draw water, water a planter and build a condenser. There are now 41 goals in all.
- The supply chest now holds 2 Bone for your first awning.

**Other**

- The version number shows on the title screen.

!!! note "Older saves"
    Saves from before 1.0 load as they were. You keep your place in the tutorial, and planters that were already growing finish their crop without needing water.
