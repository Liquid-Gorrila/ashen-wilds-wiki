# FAQ

??? question "The build ghost is red on open sand."
    Machines, stations and towers need room, so they can't go right beside another one or where one would cover another on screen. Try a cell or two to the side. It's also red on the cell you're standing on. See [Building](../building/index.md).

??? question "I can't build anything. Why is it greyed out?"
    Most structures need you to stand near a particular crafting station. The locked entry shows the station's name in brackets. Check the [tech tree](../building/index.md#the-tech-tree).

??? question "My machine isn't running."
    Look for a blinking red no-power icon. Either it's out of range of a power structure (about 6 cells), or your network is short of power. Its output buffer may also be full: empty it or add an outgoing belt. See [Power](../building/power.md).

??? question "Items are piling up at the end of my belt."
    The machine at the end won't accept that item, or it's full. Put a [Filter Belt](../building/logistics.md#sorting-with-filters) before it to divert anything it doesn't take.

??? question "Where do I get gold, cobalt and amethyst?"
    Oasis biomes have their own ores (see [biomes](../world/index.md#biomes)), caves are full of them, and quarries sometimes strike a deep seam. Merchants also trade bone for gold and amethyst.

??? question "How do I get more wood once the trees are gone?"
    The Greenhouse. It's the only renewable source of wood.

??? question "The music is too loud."
    Lower **Music volume** in Settings. It defaults to 35%, and your choice is remembered.

??? question "I died. Can I load my last save?"
    No. Death deletes the save. Every run is a fresh world.

??? question "How do I find my way home?"
    Follow the **Home** marker on the compass. It points to your workbench.

??? question "Raiders keep wrecking my base while I'm out."
    Turrets and spikes around your machines, plus the drone's free repairs, keep a base alive. Come home before night until you have them. See [Base raids](../combat/base-raids.md).

??? question "Is there an ending?"
    No. Survive as long as you can. The last tutorial goal is reaching day 10.
