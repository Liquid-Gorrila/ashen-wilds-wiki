# First days

The desert is harsh and the first days are a struggle: wood, copper and food are all scarce, and the oasis by your camp won't feed you for long. Use day 1 to get a fire and a workbench up and stay fed. Spend the next days scraping together copper for a kiln and a quarry. Once crops are growing and the quarry is digging for you, life gets much easier.

The [objectives](objectives.md) on the left of the screen walk you through the same path. If you get lost, follow the compass's **Camp** and **Home** markers.

=== "Day 1"

    1. Open the supply chest beside the tent. It holds only 6 Wood, 4 Stone, 3 Berries, 1 Copper Ore and 2 Bone.
    2. Pick the two berry bushes and fell the two small trees by camp, then range out for dead trees and rock.
    3. Build a **Campfire** (6 Wood, 3 Stone) early: cooked meat restores four times as much as raw, and animals are your best food. Stand in its light for a few seconds to become [Rested](../survival/buffs.md).
    4. Build a **Workbench** (10 Wood, 6 Stone) once you can. This is your Home marker from now on.
    5. Before noon, put up an **Awning** (3 Wood, 2 Bone) and shelter under it through the midday hours: the sun wears down your [cool](../survival/heat.md). Drink at the camp pool as you come and go.
    6. When raiders come, [roll](../combat/index.md#rolling) through their swings and use heavy chops on the tougher ones.

=== "Days 2–4"

    1. Dig a **Well** (12 Stone, 4 Wood, 2 Bone) and build two or three **Planters** (8 Wood, 3 Berries each) around it, all near the workbench. The well waters them for you, and together they keep you fed. See [Water](../survival/water.md).
    2. Ride the [Speed Racer](../world/speed-racer.md) far afield for copper and coal. Deposits are rare and give only one ore each, so copper is the bottleneck.
    3. Build a **Generator** (3 Copper Ore, 8 Stone) and a **Kiln** (14 Stone, 3 Coal) beside it, and smelt 3 Copper Bars.
    4. Build a **Quarry** (14 Stone, 8 Wood, 3 Copper Bars) inside the generator's reach. From here it digs stone and local ore for you around the clock.
    5. Build the **Component Table**, then a **Condenser** and a **Greenhouse** beside it: your only renewable wood, and steady food.

=== "Days 5+"

    1. Build a **Gun Table**, then ring your machines with **Gun Turrets**.
    2. Upgrade your sword at the **Forge**.
    3. Put [Filter Belts](../building/logistics.md#sorting-with-filters) on your quarry lines so ore flows straight into the kiln.
    4. Add **Solar Panels** and a **Battery Bank** so the base runs through the night without burning coal.
    5. Explore a [cave](../world/caves.md) for gold, cobalt and amethyst, and work toward the Automation, Advanced and Defense tables.

!!! warning "Don't wander too far at night"
    From day 1, raiders spawn around you after dark, and about a third of raid groups head for your base instead. Be home before dusk until your defenses can hold on their own. See [Base raids](../combat/base-raids.md).
