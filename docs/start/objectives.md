# Objectives

A tutorial series guides your first days. It has 41 goals in seven parts and shows one goal at a time on the left of the screen. Each goal tells you what to do, not which key to press, and completes with a chime the moment you do it.

Progress is saved with your game. You can hide the panel in [Settings](../reference/interface.md#settings).

!!! note
    A goal only counts while it is the current one, so doing something early won't tick it off later.

<div class="card" markdown>

### 1 · Stranded

1. Search the supply chest at your camp
2. Fell a tree for wood
3. Drink from the oasis pool
4. Eat some food
5. Build a workbench
6. Light a campfire

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 2 · The First Night

1. Hunt an animal
2. Cook meat over your campfire
3. Build an [awning](../survival/heat.md) for shade from the midday sun
4. Rest by your campfire
5. Survive the night

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 3 · Breaking Ground

1. Dig a [well](../survival/water.md)
2. Draw water from your well
3. Ride the Speed Racer
4. Mine copper ore (2 deposits)
5. Build a generator
6. Fuel the generator
7. Build a kiln
8. Smelt copper bars (3)
9. Build a quarry
10. Let the quarry dig something up

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 4 · Industry

1. Build a planter and water it
2. Grow and harvest vegetables
3. Build a component table
4. Craft a gear
5. Run a conveyor line into storage

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 5 · Holding the Line

1. Build a condenser to pull water from the air
2. Build a greenhouse
3. Build a gun table
4. Build a gun turret
5. Temper your blade at a forge
6. Defeat raiders (10)

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 6 · The Long Haul

1. Explore a [cave](../world/caves.md)
2. Silence a cave (defeat every warrior inside)
3. Weather a [sandstorm](../world/sandstorms.md)
4. Defeat the [Dust Jumper](../combat/boss.md)
5. Survive until day 10

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 7 · The Relics

1. Wake an [ancient relic](../world/relics.md)
2. Defeat a Relic Guardian
3. Claim a relic [boon](../combat/boons.md)
4. Defeat all three kinds of guardian

</div>
