# Objectives

A tutorial series guides your first days. It has 30 goals in six parts and shows one goal at a time on the left of the screen. Each goal tells you what to do, not which key to press, and completes with a chime the moment you do it.

Progress is saved with your game. You can hide the panel in [Settings](../reference/interface.md#settings).

!!! note
    A goal only counts while it is the current one, so doing something early won't tick it off later.

### 1 · Stranded

1. Search the supply chest at your camp
2. Fell a tree for wood
3. Eat some food
4. Build a workbench
5. Light a campfire

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 2 · The First Night

1. Hunt an animal
2. Cook meat over your campfire
3. Rest by your campfire
4. Survive the night

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 3 · Breaking Ground

1. Ride the Speed Racer
2. Mine copper ore (2 deposits)
3. Build a generator
4. Fuel the generator
5. Build a quarry
6. Let the quarry dig something up

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 4 · Industry

1. Build a kiln
2. Smelt copper bars (4)
3. Build a component table
4. Craft a gear
5. Run a conveyor line into storage

</div>

### 5 · Holding the Line

1. Build a greenhouse
2. Build a gun table
3. Build a gun turret
4. Temper your blade at a forge
5. Defeat raiders (10)

</div>

<div class="card" markdown>

### 6 · The Long Haul

4. Defeat the [Dust Jumper](../combat/boss.md)
5. Survive until day 10
