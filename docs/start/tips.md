# Tips and strategy

## Your base

- **Build at an oasis edge.** You get trees and animals close by, and open sand for machines.
- **Your structures are shade.** Within a few cells of anything you've built, desert heat stops draining extra hunger and sandstorm wind barely moves you.
- **Mistakes are free.** Dismantle a structure for a full refund of its cost plus anything stored inside.
- **Keep turret lines clear.** Turret bolts stop on trees, rocks and buildings.

## Resources

- **Spread your quarries.** A quarry digs the ores of the biome it stands in, so quarries in different oases give you different ores. See [The world](../world/index.md#biomes).
- **Scout with the racer.** Cave entrances are ringed by rich gold, cobalt and amethyst, and caves hold far more underground.
- **Bone is money.** Raiders and animals drop bone, and [merchants](../world/merchants.md) trade it for coal, gold, amethyst and cooked meat.
- **Plant early.** Planters are the first food that doesn't run out. Save berries for them instead of eating every one.
- **Copper is the bottleneck.** Every copper deposit gives just one ore, and you need about six for a generator and a quarry. Note deposits you pass and fetch them with the racer.

## Power

- **Solar plus a battery** carries a base through the night without burning fuel. Panels drop to 30% in a sandstorm, so keep a generator as backup.
- **Generators burn fuel only when needed.** They stop when demand is met and the batteries are full.
- **Use Coils** to bridge a long gap to a far quarry instead of a chain of panels.

## Survival

- **Rest before nightfall.** A few seconds by your campfire grants Rested: faster movement and stamina for 2 minutes.
- **Save Serum for raids.** Shielded halves damage, which blocks most 1-damage hits entirely.
- **Eat an Elixir for the boss.** Blazing adds +2 damage and sets targets burning.
- **Head home when the wind rises.** You get a 5-second warning before a sandstorm hits.

## Automation

- **Sort with filters.** A Filter Belt set to Copper Ore pulls copper off a quarry line so it can feed a kiln, while everything else goes on to storage.
- **Never send a machine what it won't accept.** A rejected item waits at the end of the belt and blocks everything behind it.
- **Splitters share a line.** Use one to feed two kilns, or two storage chests.
