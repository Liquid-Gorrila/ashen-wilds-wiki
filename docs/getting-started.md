# Getting started

The first night arrives about 2.5 minutes in. Use day 1 to get a workbench and a fire up, then spend the next few days building toward a quarry, a kiln and a greenhouse.

## Day 1

1. Open the starting chest (E) next to the tent.
2. Build a **Workbench** (10 Wood, 6 Stone) in or near your oasis.
3. Chop trees and hunt animals in the oasis for wood and meat. Forage berry bushes.
4. Before dusk, build a **Campfire** (6 Wood, 3 Stone), cook your meat and stay in its light.
5. When raiders come, roll through their swings and use chops on tougher targets.

## Days 2–4

1. Mine rock outcrops and deposits for stone, copper and coal. Use the [Speed Racer](speed-racer.md) to reach them quickly.
2. Build a **Generator** (3 Copper Ore, 8 Stone) and load it with coal or wood.
3. Build a **Quarry** (10 Stone, 6 Wood, 3 Copper Ore) and a **Kiln** (14 Stone, 3 Coal) beside it, inside the generator's reach.
4. Smelt 4 Copper Bars and build the **Component Table**. This unlocks the Greenhouse, solar power, batteries and belts.
5. Build a **Greenhouse**: it is your only renewable wood and a steady food supply.

## Tips

- **Base location:** build at an oasis edge, close to trees and animals but with open sand for machines.
- **Quarry placement:** a quarry yields the ores of the biome it stands in, so spread them across biomes.
- **Scouting:** caves ring their entrances with rich gold, cobalt and amethyst. Scout with the racer.
- **Saving fuel:** solar panels plus a battery bank carry a base through the night without burning coal.
- **Bone:** bone from raids buys coal, gold, amethyst and cooked meat from [merchants](merchants.md).
- **Defenses:** turrets and shockwave towers around your machines let you survive nights without standing guard.
- **Mistakes:** misplaced something? Dismantle it with X for a full refund.
