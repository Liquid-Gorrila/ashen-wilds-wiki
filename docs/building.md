# Building and the tech tree

Press C to open the build menu. Each structure is placed on the cell in front of you, shown by a ghost that turns green when the spot is free and red when it is blocked.

- **Stations gate recipes.** Most structures can only be built while you stand within reach of a particular crafting station. Locked entries show that station's name in brackets.
- **Dismantling** (X) removes the structure in front of you, or the nearest one in reach, and refunds its full cost plus anything stored inside.
- **Permanence:** your structures never despawn, and machines keep running while you are far away.

![Tech tree: Workbench leads to the Trap, Component and Alchemy tables; Component leads to the Gun and Automation tables; Automation leads to the Advanced Table, then the Defense Table](assets/images/tech-tree.png)

Build each station next to the one above it. The Component Table opens power, belts and food automation, so it is the first big goal.
