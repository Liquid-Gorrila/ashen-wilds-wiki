# Companion drone

A small green drone follows you from the start of every run. It lights the way at night and helps out on its own:

| Job | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zap | A raider is within about 8 cells of the drone and near you | 1 damage every 1.4 s |
| Repair | You are at your base, a building near you is damaged, and no raider is close | Flies to the building and restores 1 health every 1.2 s |

Fighting always comes first: the drone drops a repair job the moment a raider shows up. It does nothing while you ride the [Speed Racer](speed-racer.md).
