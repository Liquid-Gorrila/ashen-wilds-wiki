# Speed Racer

The Speed Racer is a hover-bike that crosses the desert about 2.5 times faster than walking. One is always parked at your starting camp; ruins and merchant camps sometimes have another.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Board / get off | E |
| Top speed | 185 (walking is 72) |
| Fuel use | About 2.2% per second at full throttle, roughly 45 s of flat-out driving per full tank |
| Empty tank | Crawls at 40 |
| Refuelling | Automatic when you board: each Fuel adds 35%, each Coal 12%, up to about 92% |
| Starting fuel | 45% at the start camp, 20–60% when found elsewhere |

- **Ramming:** driving into a raider or animal at speed deals 2 damage, or 3 near top speed, and slows you down.
- **Crashing:** hitting a tree, rock or building hard costs most of your speed.
- **Limits:** racers cannot cross chasms, and raiders can still hurt you while you ride.
- **Where you leave it:** a racer stays exactly where you park it, and its headlight helps at night.
