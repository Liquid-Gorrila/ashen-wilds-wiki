# Objectives

A tutorial series guides your first days. It is split into six parts and shows one goal at a time on the left of the screen. Each goal completes the moment you do the thing it describes, with a chime. Progress is saved with your game, and the panel can be hidden in [Settings](interface.md#settings).

## Part 1: Stranded

1. Search the supply chest at your camp
2. Fell a tree for wood
3. Eat some food
4. Build a workbench
5. Light a campfire

## Part 2: The First Night

1. Hunt an animal
2. Cook meat over your campfire
3. Rest by your campfire
4. Survive the night

## Part 3: Breaking Ground

1. Ride the Speed Racer
2. Mine copper ore (2 deposits)
3. Build a generator
4. Fuel the generator
5. Build a quarry
6. Let the quarry dig something up

## Part 4: Industry

1. Build a kiln
2. Smelt copper bars (4)
3. Build a component table
4. Craft a gear
5. Run a conveyor line into storage

## Part 5: Holding the Line

1. Build a greenhouse
2. Build a gun table
3. Build a gun turret
4. Temper your blade at a forge
5. Defeat raiders (10)

## Part 6: The Long Haul

1. Explore a [cave](caves.md)
2. Silence a cave (defeat every warrior inside)
3. Weather a [sandstorm](sandstorms.md)
4. Defeat the [Dust Jumper](combat.md#the-dust-jumper-boss)
5. Survive until day 10

Goals only count while they are the current goal, so doing something early does not tick it off later.
