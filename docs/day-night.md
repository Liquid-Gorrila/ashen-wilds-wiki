# Day and night

A full day lasts 4 minutes. Night takes roughly the last quarter of it, and each night the raids grow larger and tougher.

| Phase | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dawn | about 25 s | Light returns, a bell rings and the game [autosaves](saving.md). A new day begins and the day counter goes up. |
| Morning and afternoon | about 2 min 15 s | Safe to explore. Desert heat is active. From day 2, a pair of raiders may wander in, and a [sandstorm](sandstorms.md) may blow. |
| Dusk | about 25 s | The sky reddens. "Night falls" warns you raids are coming. |
| Night | about 65 s | A horn sounds and the music darkens. Lamps, fires and machine lights matter. Raiders spawn in waves near you. |

You start on the morning of day 1, and day 1 has no daytime raiders.

## How night raids escalate

| Day | New raiders in the pool | Wave size | Most raiders at once |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warrior, Archer, Tribe Hunter | 1–2 | 6 |
| 2 | Spear Guard, Tribe Brute | 1–3 | 8 |
| 3 | Dust Jumper can appear (35% chance, once a night) | 1–4 | 10 |
| 4+ | Tamed Beast | 1–4 | 12, rising by 2 a day to 16 |

Waves spawn 170–220 pixels from you, every 3–7 seconds; the gap shrinks each day. Raiders who fall far behind you give up and vanish.

See [Combat and enemies](combat.md) for what each raider does.
