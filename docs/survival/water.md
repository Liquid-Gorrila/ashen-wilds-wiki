# Water

Water keeps you alive, cools you down and grows your food. Early on you depend on the oases. By mid-game your base makes its own.

| Source | How much | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| :material-waves: Oasis pool | Unlimited | Use one (++e++) to drink your fill, cool off completely, become [Refreshed](buffs.md) and fill up to 3 waterskins. A **sandstorm silts pools up**: you can still drink, but you can't fill waterskins or get Refreshed. |
| :material-water-well: Well | 1 Water every 60 s in open desert, every 35 s in an oasis | Holds up to 4. Needs no power. Use it to collect the water. |
| :material-water-pump: Condenser | 1 Water every 18 s by day, every 9 s at night | Holds up to 6. Needs 1 power. Pulls water from the cool night air, and only manages half speed in a sandstorm. |

Water from a well or condenser isn't limited to three waterskins, so you can stockpile it. Both push water onto any [conveyor](../building/logistics.md) leading away from them.

## Watering crops

- **Planters** need 1 Water to grow a crop. That one watering lasts all three growth stages, through to harvest. A dry planter looks pale and won't grow. Use it while carrying Water to water it, or deliver Water by belt (it holds 2 in reserve).
- **Greenhouses** use 1 Water each time they grow something. Load it (++e++), belt it in, or put a water source nearby.
- **Irrigation:** any planter or greenhouse within about 4 tiles (64 px) of a well or condenser draws water from it by itself. Build a well among your planters and they'll look after themselves.

## Drinking

- Each Water restores 45 thirst and 20 cool. Berries, vegetables, serum and elixir restore a little thirst too. See [Food](food.md).
- ++f++ drinks when you're thirstier than you are hungry.
