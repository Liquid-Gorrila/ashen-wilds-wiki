# Heat and shade

The midday sun is as dangerous as any raider. A fifth meter, **Cool** (the sun icon), shows how well you're holding up against the heat. It refills on its own and only drains while you stand in the open around noon.

## The Cool meter

| Where you are | Cool per second | From full to empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midday sun, open ground | −0.6 | about 2¾ minutes |
| Midday sun, scorching ground (Desert, Scorched Badlands, Bloodfields) | −1.3 | about 77 seconds |
| Outside the midday hours, or in a sandstorm | +0.8 | |
| In shade by day | +1.2 | |
| At night | +1.2 | |
| In a cave | +1.6 | |

The **midday hours** run from mid-morning to mid-afternoon, about 40% of each day. The biome label in the top right says "midday sun" or "scorching" while the sun is wearing you down, and "in shade" when you're out of it. A sandstorm's dust blocks the sun, though it brings troubles of its own.

Drinking Water restores 20 cool, and drinking your fill at an oasis pool cools you completely.

## Ailments

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-white-balance-sunny:{ .lg .middle } **Overheated**

    ---

    **When:** cool is at a quarter or less.

    **Effect:** stamina refills at half speed and thirst drains 40% faster.

-   :material-thermometer-alert:{ .lg .middle } **Heatstroke**

    ---

    **When:** cool is empty.

    **Effect:** you lose 1 health every 5 seconds.

</div>

Both last exactly as long as their cause, so they show no countdown. Get into shade and they clear as your cool recovers.

## Finding shade

- **Awning:** stand within about 2 tiles of one. It costs only 3 Wood and 2 Bone, needs no station, and is your first line of defence on day one. The supply chest at camp holds the bone.
- **Tall buildings:** stand right up against a workbench, machine or other tall structure.
- **Caves** are the coolest places of all.

!!! tip "Plan your day around noon"
    Scavenge at dawn and dusk, and spend the midday hours building at home under an awning. If you must cross open desert at noon, drink as you go: each Water buys back 20 cool.
