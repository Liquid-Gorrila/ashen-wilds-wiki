# Defense and weapons

Automated defenses protect your base at night while you work elsewhere. All except spikes need [power](power.md), and only draw it while a raider is in range.

| Defense | Power | Range | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spike Trap | None | Its own cell | 1 damage every 0.5 s to raiders standing on it. Walkable. |
| Gun Turret | 1 | about 11 cells | Aims in four directions and fires a bolt every 0.8 s for 2 damage. |
| Flamethrower | 2 | about 5 cells, left or right | Burns raiders in a narrow line for 1 damage every 0.25 s. Choose its side with R when placing. |
| Shockwave Tower | 3 | about 6 cells all around | Every 3.5 s, a blast deals 3 damage to every raider in range with heavy knockback. |

Turret bolts stop on trees, rocks and buildings, so keep its lines of fire clear.

## Sword upgrades

Each upgrade adds +1 damage to every slash and chop, for up to +5. Tiers must be bought in order; press E at the right forge.

| Tier | Upgrade | Cost | Forge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Copper Edge | 6 Copper Ore, 2 Coal | Forge |
| 2 | Golden Edge | 6 Gold Ore, 2 Amethyst | Forge |
| 3 | Cobalt Edge | 8 Cobalt Ore, 4 Amethyst | Forge |
| 4 | Tempered Cobalt | 6 Cobalt Bar, 2 Circuit | Advanced Forge |
| 5 | Starforged Blade | 6 Gold Bar, 6 Cobalt Bar, 1 Elixir | Advanced Forge |
