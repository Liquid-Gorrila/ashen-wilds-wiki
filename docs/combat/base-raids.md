# Base raids

Raiders don't just come for you. Melee raiders attack any of your buildings that is closer to them than you are. Archers and Tribe Hunters only shoot at you.

![A night raid led by a Dust Warrior closing on a lit base](../assets/images/raid.png)

## Sieges

At night, about a third of raid groups go straight for your base while you're away from it but still in the area. The first siege each night warns you: **"Raiders are attacking your base!"** These raiders hunt buildings first and only turn on you up close.

Turrets, spikes, flamethrowers, shockwave towers and your [companion drone](companion.md) defend the base while you're gone. See [Defenses](../building/defense.md).

## Building health

A damaged building shows a health bar and flashes red when hit.

| Structures | Health |
| --- | --- |
| Belts, filters, splitters, crossers, transporters, spikes | Can't be attacked |
| Planter | 10 |
| Campfire | 15 |
| Generator, Solar Panel, Battery Bank, Coil | 25 |
| Stations, machines, Storage, forges | 30 |
| Gun Turret, Flamethrower | 40 |
| Shockwave Tower | 50 |

The [Dust Jumper](boss.md)'s slam deals 5 damage to every building it lands near.

## Repair and salvage

Repair
:   Use a damaged building to repair it. It costs a quarter of the build cost, scaled by how badly it's damaged, and always at least 1 of its first material. Your [drone](companion.md) also mends buildings for free while you're at base.

Destroyed
:   A building at 0 health is gone for good. It leaves a third of its build cost and half of what it held as salvage on the ground.
