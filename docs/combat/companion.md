# Companion drone

A small green drone follows you from the start of every run. It lights the way at night and helps out on its own.

| Job | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| :material-flash: Zap | A raider is within about 8 cells of the drone and near you | 1 damage every 1.4 s |
| :material-wrench: Repair | You're at your base, a building near you is damaged, and no raider is close | Flies to the building and restores 1 health every 1.2 s |

- **Fighting comes first.** The drone drops a repair job the moment a raider shows up.
- **Repairs are free.** No materials are used, unlike repairing by hand.
- **It rests while you drive.** The drone does nothing while you ride the [Speed Racer](../world/speed-racer.md).
