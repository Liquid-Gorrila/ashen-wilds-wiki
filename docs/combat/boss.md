# The Dust Jumper

From day 3, the Dust Jumper may hunt you once a night (35% chance). It has **40 health**, shown in a bar at the top of the screen, and the compass shows a red **Boss** marker while it's after you.

## Attacks

Swipe
:   Up close, it swipes for 2 damage.

Leap and slam
:   Every few seconds it jumps out of sight. A target marker chases you, locks in place for a moment, then the boss crashes down for 3 damage around it. Roll or run out of the marker as it locks. It can't be hurt while airborne.

Rage
:   Below half health it leaps more often, and calls in two Warriors after each slam.

!!! danger "Keep it away from your base"
    Every slam deals 5 damage to each building it lands near. Leading the fight away from your machines can save you a lot of repairs.

## Reward

5 Gold Ore, 4 Amethyst and 4 Cobalt Ore. Defeating it is one of the [objectives](../start/objectives.md) in part 6.

!!! tip "Preparing for the fight"
    Eat an Elixir for Blazing (+2 damage and burning), and keep a Serum for Shielded. Upgraded swords make a big difference: at Starforged Blade, every chop deals 8.
