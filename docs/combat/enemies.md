# Raiders and wildlife

## Raiders

Raiders come at night, and in pairs during the day from day 2. Tougher ones join the pool as the days go by: see [Day and night](../world/day-night.md#how-night-raids-escalate).

| Raider | Health | Damage | Behaviour | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | 3 | 1 | Charges in and swings | 0–1 Bone |
| Archer | 2 | 1 | Keeps its distance and fires arrows | 0–1 Bone |
| Tribe Hunter | 4 | 1 | Ranged bowman; backs away if you close in | 1–2 Bone |
| Spear Guard | 6 | 2 | Slow, long-reach spear thrust | 1 Bone, 0–1 Copper Ore |
| Tribe Brute | 9 | 2 | Heavy melee; light hits don't interrupt it | 1–3 Bone, 0–1 Gold Ore |
| Tamed Beast | 18 | 3 | Huge, fast melee; shrugs off light hits | 3–5 Bone, 2–3 Raw Meat, 0–1 Amethyst |

!!! tip "Dealing with heavies"
    Slashes still hurt Brutes and Tamed Beasts but won't stagger them. Roll through their swing, then answer with a chop.

## Wildlife

Animals live in oases and wander near where they spawned. Sheep and chickens travel in pairs.

| Animal | Health | Behaviour | Drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rabbit | 1 | Flees fast | 1 Raw Meat |
| Chicken | 1 | Flees | 1 Raw Meat, 0–1 Bone |
| Sheep | 3 | Flees when hit | 2 Raw Meat, 0–1 Bone |
| Boar | 5 | Turns and charges when hit, for 1 damage | 2–3 Raw Meat, 1–2 Bone |
