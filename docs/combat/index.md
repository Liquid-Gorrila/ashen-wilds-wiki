# Combat

Your only weapon is the sword. The same strikes that fight raiders also fell trees, break rocks and mine deposits.

| Attack | Damage | Stamina | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slash | 1 | Free | Forward arc. Hold the button to chain slashes. |
| Chop | 3 | 4 | Wider arc with heavy knockback. Hits harder on trees and rocks too. |

Attacks aim at the mouse cursor when you use the mouse, otherwise in the direction you're moving or facing. Each [forge upgrade](../building/defense.md#sword-upgrades) adds +1 to both attacks, up to +5, and the [Blazing](../survival/buffs.md) buff adds +2 and sets targets on fire.

## Rolling

A roll costs 3 stamina and makes you invulnerable for 0.35 s: enough to pass through most swings, arrows and the Dust Jumper's slam. With 11 stamina you get three rolls back to back, so don't spend them all on chops.
