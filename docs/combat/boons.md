# Relic boons

Each time you defeat a [Relic Guardian](../world/relics.md), you choose one of three boons. Boons are permanent: they work on their own, on their own timers, so a few of them stack into a build of their own.

Choosing a boon you already have ranks it up, to **rank III** at most. Each rank adds damage, and each rank shortens its cooldown by 15%. Boons are saved with your game, and the pause menu lists the ones you have.

Boons hit raiders, elites, bosses and hostile wildlife, but never your buildings or tame animals. They rest while you ride the Speed Racer. If nothing is in range, a boon waits and checks again a moment later instead of using up its cooldown.

| Boon | What it does | Cooldown (rank I) | Damage at rank I / II / III |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boomerang Axe | Hurls an axe at the nearest foe within ~10 m. It flies back to you and can hit each foe once each way. | 3 s | 2 / 3 / 4 |
| Techno Blade | One blade per rank orbits you, cutting each foe it touches at most twice a second. | Always on | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Chain Lightning | Strikes the nearest foe within ~8 m, then leaps to more foes nearby: 3 / 4 / 5 targets in all. | 4 s | 2 / 3 / 4 each |
| Ice Spikes | Spikes burst up under 2 / 3 / 4 random foes within ~7 m. | 5 s | 2 / 3 / 4 each |
| Fireball | Launches a fireball at the nearest foe within ~11 m. It explodes where they stood and sets everything caught on fire. | 3.5 s | 3 / 4 / 5 |
| Rune Circle | When foes come close, a rune spins beneath you for 3 seconds, hurting everything inside every 0.5 s. | 7 s | 1 / 2 / 3 per pulse |
| Missile Strike | Finds the thickest crowd within ~13 m, marks it, and drops a missile 0.8 s later. Sets survivors on fire. | 14 s | 5 / 7 / 9 |

!!! tip "Good first picks"
    Techno Blade and Boomerang Axe help in every fight. Missile Strike and Rune Circle shine during night waves and [base raids](base-raids.md), when raiders bunch up.
