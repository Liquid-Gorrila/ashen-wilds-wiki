# Production machines

Powered machines work in cycles, holding up to 12 items in their input and output buffers. A full output buffer stops the machine until it is emptied.

- **Feeding them:** press E to load inputs from your inventory, or deliver them by [conveyor](logistics.md).
- **Emptying them:** press E to collect the output, or place a conveyor next to the machine pointing away from it. Machines push finished goods onto any such belt automatically.

| Machine | Cycle | Power | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarry | 7 s | 2 | None | Stone (about 70%) or a local ore (about 30%); 4% chance of a deep seam (gold, cobalt or amethyst) |
| Advanced Quarry | 4 s | 3 | None | Local ore (60%), coal (15%) or stone; 10% chance of a deep seam |
| Kiln | 5 s | 2 | 1 Copper, Gold or Cobalt Ore | 1 matching bar |
| Fuel Refinery | 6 s | 2 | 2 Coal | 1 Fuel |
| Greenhouse | 12 s | 1 | None | 2 Berries (40%), 1 Vegetables (30%) or 2 Wood (30%) |
| Bio Lab | 8 s | 2 | 1 Vegetables + 1 Bone | 1 Serum |

A quarry's "local ore" depends on the biome it stands in (see [The world](world.md#biomes)). Build quarries in different biomes to get different ores.

## Unpowered production

| Structure | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Planter | Grows a random crop through three stages, about 22 s each. Press E when ripe for 2 Vegetables. |
| Campfire | Press E to cook up to 5 Raw Meat into Cooked Meat. Also lights the area. |
| Forge, Advanced Forge | Upgrade your sword (see [Sword upgrades](defense.md#sword-upgrades)). |
