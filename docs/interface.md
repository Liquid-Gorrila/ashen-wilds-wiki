# Interface and menus

Everything on screen, and every menu, at a glance.

## The HUD

| Where | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Top left | Health bar, stamina bar and hunger bar (orange; turns red below a quarter) |
| Under the bars | Active [buffs](buffs.md) with their seconds remaining |
| Left side | Your current [objective](objectives.md) and its chapter |
| Top centre | The compass (see below) |
| Top right | Day number, time of day, and the biome you are standing in ("scorching" in desert heat, "Sandstorm!" during a storm) |
| Left, lower | Item pick-up notices |
| Bottom centre | What you can use nearby, then your inventory bar |

## Compass

The compass ribbon covers a full circle, with north (screen up) in the middle and east and west to either side. Markers sit at their direction from you, labelled with their distance in cells.

| Marker | Points to |
| --- | --- |
| Home (green) | Your workbench, or your first campfire if you have no workbench yet |
| Camp (amber) | The starting camp. Hidden once Home is right next to it. |
| Racer (red-orange) | The nearest Speed Racer nobody is riding |
| Boss (red) | The Dust Jumper, while it is hunting you |

Markers you are standing on disappear. When two labels would overlap, one drops to a second line.

## Title menu

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Continue (day N) | Loads your saved run. Only shown when a save exists. |
| New game | Starts a fresh world and deletes any old save. |
| Settings | Opens the settings screen. |
| Quit | Closes the game. |

## Pause menu

Press Esc or P during play.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Resume | Back to the game |
| Save game | Saves now, then resumes |
| Settings | Opens the settings screen |
| Save and quit to title | Saves, then returns to the title menu |

## Settings

Settings are remembered between sessions.

| Setting | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Master volume | All sound |
| Music volume | The day and night themes |
| Effects volume | Combat, machines, wind, menus and everything else |
| Fullscreen | Switches between fullscreen and windowed |
| Screen shake | Turns camera shake on hits and explosions on or off |
| Show FPS | Frame-rate counter in the top right (F3 also toggles it) |
| Show compass | Hides or shows the compass |
| Show objectives | Hides or shows the objectives panel |

Menus work with W/S or the arrow keys to move, E or Enter to choose, A/D to adjust sliders, and the mouse.
