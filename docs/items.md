# Items and how to get them

Every item has at least one renewable source through automation. Wild sources run out, so plan to replace each one with a machine.

Strike trees, deposits and rocks with your sword; a chop deals more damage than a slash. Press E on bushes, chests, merchants and machines.

## Raw materials

| Item | Wild sources (finite) | Renewable sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | Trees (1–3, big trees 3–5), dead trees (1–2), chests | Greenhouse (2 per harvest) |
| Stone | Rock blocks (1–3), stone deposits (2–3), other deposits (0–1) | Quarry, Advanced Quarry |
| Coal | Coal deposits (1–2) | Quarry in Desert or Badlands, Advanced Quarry, merchant |
| Copper Ore | Copper deposits (1–2), Spear Guard drops | Quarry where copper occurs |
| Gold Ore | Gold deposits, Tribe Brute drops, rich chests, Dust Jumper | Quarry deep seams, merchant |
| Cobalt Ore | Cobalt deposits, cave ore, Dust Jumper | Quarry deep seams |
| Amethyst | Amethyst deposits, cave ore, Tamed Beast drops, Dust Jumper | Quarry deep seams, merchant |
| Bone | Bone stalks and totems, animals, most raiders | Hunting and raids |

A Basic Quarry has a 4% chance per cycle to strike a deep seam of gold, cobalt or amethyst anywhere; the Advanced Quarry has 10%.

## Processed materials

| Item | Made at | Input |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copper Bar | Kiln | 1 Copper Ore |
| Gold Bar | Kiln | 1 Gold Ore |
| Cobalt Bar | Kiln | 1 Cobalt Ore |
| Fuel | Fuel Refinery | 2 Coal |
| Gear | Component Table (press E) | 1 Copper Bar |
| Circuit | Automation Table (press E) | 1 Gold Bar + 1 Gear |

## Food

| Item | Sources |
| --- | --- |
| Berries | Berry bushes in oases (2–3, regrow after 4 minutes), Greenhouse, merchant |
| Vegetables | Planter (2 per harvest), Greenhouse |
| Raw Meat | Sheep, boars, rabbits, chickens, Tamed Beasts |
| Cooked Meat | Campfire (cooks up to 5 raw meat per use), chests, merchants |
| Serum | Bio Lab: 1 Vegetables + 1 Bone |
| Elixir | Alchemy Table (press E): 3 Berries + 1 Amethyst |

How much each food restores is listed under [Survival](survival.md#food).
