# Day and night

A full day lasts 4 minutes. Night takes about the last quarter of it, and each night the raids grow larger and tougher.

![A lit base at night](../assets/images/night.png)

| Phase | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| :material-weather-sunset-up: Dawn | about 25 s | Light returns and a bell rings. The day counter goes up and the game [autosaves](../reference/saving.md). |
| :material-white-balance-sunny: Day | about 2 min 15 s | Safe to explore, but the desert is hot. From day 2 a [sandstorm](sandstorms.md) may blow, and from day 3 a pair of raiders may wander in. |
| :material-weather-sunset-down: Dusk | about 25 s | The sky reddens, and "Night falls" warns you that raids are coming. |
| :material-weather-night: Night | about 65 s | A horn sounds and the music darkens. Raiders spawn in waves near you. Lamps, fires and machine lights matter. |

You start on the morning of day 1, and day 1 has no daytime raiders.

## How night raids escalate

The first nights are a handful of raiders at a time. The pressure builds over the first week, giving you time to get planters, a quarry and then turrets going before the elites and the boss arrive.

| Day | New in the raider pool | Group size | Most raiders at once | A wave every |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Warrior, Archer | 1 | 3 | 8 s |
| 2 | Tribe Hunter | 1–2 | 4 | 7.5 s |
| 3 | | 1–2 | 5 | 7 s |
| 4 | Spear Guard | 1–2 | 6 | 6.5 s |
| 5 | Tribe Brute. The [Dust Jumper](../combat/boss.md) may appear (35% chance, rolled as night falls). | 1–3 | 7 | 6 s |
| 6 | About one wave in five is led by a [Dust Warrior](../combat/enemies.md#elites) | 1–3 | 8 | 5.5 s |
| 7 | Tamed Beast | 1–3 | 9 | 5 s |
| 8+ | [Electrocutioners](../combat/enemies.md#elites) can lead waves too | 1–4 | 10, then +1 a day up to 16 | 4.5 s, down to 3.5 s |

Groups spawn just off screen, and a group never takes the count past the night's limit. Raiders who fall far behind you give up and vanish.

Some groups ignore you and go for your base instead. See [Base raids](../combat/base-raids.md).
