# Relics and guardians

Ancient relics are machines left behind by whoever lived here before the sand. Each one sleeps at the heart of overgrown temple ruins, guarded by a **Relic Guardian**. Beat the guardian and the relic grants you a permanent [boon](../combat/boons.md).

## Finding a relic

Relic sites are a rare [landmark](index.md#landmarks), never in the regions right next to your starting camp. You'll know one by its ring of temple ruins: mossy statues, stone urns, broken pillars and rubble, with a silver loot chest at the back.

The compass shows a lilac **Relic** marker for the nearest relic you haven't beaten yet, up to three regions away.

There are three kinds of relic, each with its own guardian:

| Relic | Glow | Guardian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relic of the Warden | Cyan | [The Warden](#the-warden) |
| Relic of the Pyre | Fire orange | [The Pyre](#the-pyre) |
| Relic of the Hollow | Pink | [The Hollow](#the-hollow) |

## Waking a relic

Stand next to the relic and use it (++e++) to wake it. It powers up, and its guardian appears. The fight uses the boss bar at the top of the screen.

Guardians stay near their relic. If you run more than about 28 m away and stay away for 4 seconds, the guardian goes back to sleep and the relic goes dark. The same happens if you go down into a cave. You can wake it again whenever you're ready.

When the guardian falls, the relic pours out its power and depletes for good. You get:

- 4 Gold Ore, 3 Amethyst, 3 Cobalt Ore and 2 Cooked Meat
- a choice of three [boons](../combat/boons.md)

!!! info "All guardians speed up"
    Below half health, every guardian attacks about 30% more often.

## The Warden

**60 health.** A heavy stone sentinel that comes crashing out of the sky when its relic wakes. Stay out from under it as it lands.

Ground slam
:   Up close, it charges an orb and slams the ground for 2 damage around it.

Beam
:   It fires a beam that sweeps diagonally downward and then straight down, for 2 damage per sweep. Move above it or to the side.

Rush
:   It crouches for half a second, then dashes in a straight line, dealing 2 damage if it runs into you. Roll sideways when it crouches.

Leap
:   It leaps out of sight. A marker chases you and locks just before the Warden lands for 3 damage. The landing also deals 5 damage to any building it lands near.

Mend
:   Once per fight, below half health, it heals 15.

## The Pyre

**70 health.** A slow, smouldering brute that controls space with fire.

Fire rings
:   It launches two rings of eight fire orbs, one after the other. Each orb deals 1 damage. Step into the gaps between them.

Flamethrower
:   It aims a jet of fire in one of eight directions and holds it for about 2 seconds. Standing in it deals 1 damage every 0.3 s and sets you on fire. Get out of the line, not just further away.

Burning slam
:   Up close, it slams the ground for 2 damage and sets you on fire.

## The Hollow

**55 health.** A hooded caster that floats out of reach and keeps you moving.

Falling swords
:   It drops three spectral swords: one on where you're standing and two beside it. A ground marker shows each one before it lands for 2 damage.

Daggers
:   It summons three daggers around you. Each one hovers, spins at you (1 damage) and slices (1 damage), twice before it vanishes. A dagger breaks after 2 hits, and the Hollow keeps at most three in the air.

Ward
:   Below three-quarters health it wards itself for 6 seconds, halving the damage it takes. It can ward again every 18 seconds.

!!! tip "Fighting the Hollow"
    It backs away if you close in. A [Speed Racer](speed-racer.md) or the [Rune Circle](../combat/boons.md) boon helps pin it down, and the Ward is the moment to deal with the daggers instead.
