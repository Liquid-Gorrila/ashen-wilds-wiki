# Merchants and loot

## Merchants

Merchants stand at merchant camps. Each one offers a single fixed trade, which you can make as often as you can pay.

| You give | You get |
| --- | --- |
| 8 Wood | 5 Berries |
| 4 Bone | 2 Cooked Meat |
| 10 Stone | 3 Coal |
| 4 Copper Ore | 1 Gold Ore |
| 6 Bone | 2 Amethyst |
| 6 Berries | 2 Cooked Meat |

!!! tip "Bone is the currency"
    Bone comes from raiders, animals and bone totems, so every night raid pays for trades.

## Loot chests

Chests open once and never refill.

| Chest | Found at | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supply chest | Your starting camp | 14 Wood, 10 Stone, 6 Berries, 3 Copper Ore, 4 Coal |
| Loot chest | Camps, ruins, bone fields | 3–5 of the local biome's main ore, 1–2 Cooked Meat, 4 Wood |
| Rich chest | Tribe camps and caves | 3 Gold Ore, 2 Amethyst, 3 Cooked Meat |
