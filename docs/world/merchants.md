# Merchants and loot

## Merchants

Merchants stand at merchant camps. Each one offers a single fixed trade, which you can make as often as you can pay.

| You give | You get |
| --- | --- |
| 10 Wood | 3 Berries |
| 5 Bone | 1 Cooked Meat |
| 12 Stone | 2 Coal |
| 4 Copper Ore | 1 Gold Ore |
| 6 Bone | 2 Amethyst |
| 8 Berries | 2 Cooked Meat |

!!! tip "Bone is the currency"
    Bone comes from raiders, animals and bone totems, so every night raid pays for trades.

## Loot chests

Chests open once and never refill. Their colour tells you how good they are: bronze, silver or gold. They come in four shapes, but the shape doesn't change what's inside.

| Chest | Found at | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supply chest (bronze) | Your starting camp | 6 Wood, 4 Stone, 3 Berries, 1 Copper Ore |
| Bronze chest | Camps, bone fields | 2–3 of the local biome's main ore, 1 Cooked Meat, 2 Wood |
| Silver chest | Ruins, relic sites | 3–5 of the local biome's main ore, 1 of its other ore, 2 Coal, 1 Cooked Meat |
| Gold chest | Tribe camps and caves | 3 Gold Ore, 2 Amethyst, 3 Cooked Meat |
