# Sandstorms

Sandstorms are daytime weather events that start from day 2. At most one blows each day.

## How a storm plays out

| Stage | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warning | 5 s | "The wind is rising..." and a low rumble. The air starts to haze. |
| Storm | about 50 s | "Sandstorm!" Thick dusty haze, sand streaking across the screen, howling wind. |
| Easing | a few seconds | The wind dies down and "The sandstorm passes." |

## Effects

- **Wind push:** the wind blows you, raiders and the Speed Racer in one direction, which drifts slowly during the storm. Walking into it is slow; walking with it is fast.
- **Visibility:** the light turns dim and orange.
- **Solar power:** solar panels produce only 30% while the storm blows.

## Shelter

Stay within a few cells of your own structures and the wind barely touches you (15% of its strength). Riding out a storm at your base is the safest choice.

Weathering a sandstorm is one of the [objectives](objectives.md) in Part 6.
